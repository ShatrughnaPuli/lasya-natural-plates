How It's Made

From Fallen Leaf to Premium Tableware

Our sustainable, zero-chemical manufacturing process preserves natural textures while ensuring supreme hygiene and strength.

1

Fallen Leaf Collection

Naturally shed areca leaves are ethically hand-gathered from organic plantations.

2

High-Pressure Wash

Cleaned with fresh pressurized spring water without any chemicals or bleach.

3

Hydraulic Heat Press

Precision heated steel molds shape the leaf into rigid, leak-proof designs.

4

UV & Quality Inspection

Each plate is edge-trimmed, UV sterilized, and inspected for structural perfection.

5

Moisture-Free Pack

Sealed in export-grade shrink wrap with silica packets for worldwide transit.
